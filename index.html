Story & Time Loops

*Dark* is a German science fiction thriller that delves into the complexities of time travel, parallel worlds, and existential mysteries. Set in the small town of Winden, the story unfolds across multiple timelines, revealing how actions in the past, present, and future are interconnected.

Key Elements:

  • Time Loops: Characters experience loops where events repeat, influenced by a wormhole (the "passage") that allows travel between 1953, 1986, and 2019. The "knot" represents the tangled timelines that must be unraveled to prevent an apocalypse.
  • Parallel Worlds: The show introduces alternate realities, where characters exist in different versions, leading to paradoxes and moral dilemmas.
  • The Apocalypse: A catastrophic event threatens Winden, tied to nuclear power, family curses, and the enigmatic Sic Mundus Creatus Est (SMC) organization.

Characters

Jonas Kahnwald

A troubled teenager who discovers time travel after his father's disappearance. He becomes entangled in the loops, seeking to save his loved ones while grappling with the consequences of altering time.

Martha Nielsen

Jonas's love interest, who experiences parallel versions of herself. Her story explores identity, loss, and the ethical dilemmas of time manipulation.

Ulrich Nielsen

A police officer obsessed with solving disappearances in Winden. His investigations reveal family secrets spanning generations and timelines.

Hannah Kahnwald

Michael's wife and Jonas's mother, involved in a web of affairs and mysteries. Her actions in different eras drive key plot points.

Egon Tiedemann

A physicist and founder of the time travel experiments. His past self influences the present, uncovering the origins of the wormhole.

Mikkel Nielsen

Ulrich's son, who vanishes and reappears in the past as Michael Kahnwald, creating a loop that affects multiple generations.

Katharina Nielsen

Ulrich's wife and a teacher, who uncovers hidden truths about Winden's history and her family's role in the time anomalies.

Helge Dohrendorf

A reclusive, mentally challenged man with a dark past, connected to the Tiedemann family and the origins of the time travel device.

Claudia Tiedemann

Egon's wife and a nurse, who becomes a key figure in unraveling the mysteries, spanning timelines with her knowledge of the loops.

Noah

Noah, also known as Hanno Tauber, was a dedicated follower of Sic Mundus. He was the son of Bartosz and Silja Tiedemann and the brother of Agnes Nielsen. Following the apocalypse, he was in a relationship with Elisabeth Doppler. They had a daughter, Charlotte, who was kidnapped as a baby.
